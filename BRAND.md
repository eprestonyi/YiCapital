# YiCapital fixed brand standard

The YiCapital logo is the frozen nonlinear-pendulum identity.

## Master geometry

- Five hard components: square pivot, first square-ended arm, square joint,
  second square-ended arm, and square terminal mass.
- The terminal mass is solid blue.
- One continuous open blue arc starts at the terminal mass and rises around the
  right side. It never closes, crosses itself, loops, or becomes a circle.
- The wordmark is `YiCapital`, with no space and no gradient.
- The symbol sits to the left of the wordmark. Compact surfaces may use the
  symbol alone.

## Color

- Dark surfaces: structure and wordmark `#F5F2EA`; arc and terminal `#75A7FF`.
- Light surfaces: structure and wordmark `#070A0F`; arc and terminal `#5F8CFF`.
- Do not use the former blue-purple gradient in a logo.

## Canonical assets

- Dark-surface symbol: `/assets/brand/yicapital-mark-reverse.svg`
- Light-surface symbol: `/assets/brand/yicapital-mark-dark.svg`
- Small app/browser mark: `/favicon.svg`

Website lockups keep the accessible `YiCapital` text in HTML and attach the
canonical symbol through shared CSS. The HTML text must remain one line and use
a solid wordmark color matching the surface. Product names in prose, such as
`Yi Capital US`, are not logo wordmarks and retain their established names.
